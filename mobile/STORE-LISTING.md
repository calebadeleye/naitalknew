## Full description

Green Mail is your business email on your phone: the webmail from NAI TALK, built for your
own domain.

• Read and write email from any address on your NAI TALK mailbox, with several accounts and
  one-tap switching
• Inbox with All, Unread, Flagged and Attachments tabs, search, star, archive, spam and delete
• Rich-text writing: bold, italic, underline, lists, links, templates and signatures
• Attach files and photos, schedule an email to send later
• Works offline: read and search saved mail, write drafts, and send from the outbox once you
  are back online. You always see what is waiting or failed
• Calendar, Tasks, Notes, Filters and automatic replies in one place
• Smart assistant on request: summarise an email, draft a reply, list action items or
  translate to Yoruba
• Notifications when new mail arrives
• Remote images are blocked until you allow them, and emails open in a locked-down viewer
• Light and dark themes

You need a Green Mail / NAI TALK email address. Get one at www.naitalk.com/green-mail.

## Google Play — Data safety

Collected and shared: **no data shared with third parties** for advertising or analytics.
Collected (processed on NAI TALK's servers to provide the service), all encrypted in transit,
user can request deletion by contacting NAI TALK:

| Type | Why | Optional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email address | account sign-in | required |
| Emails / messages, calendar events, tasks, notes, contacts you add | app functionality | required for the feature |
| Photos and files you attach | app functionality (only what you pick) | optional |
| Device or other IDs (push notification token) | notifications | optional |

Not collected: location, contacts from the phone, call logs, health, financial info, browsing
history. App activity/analytics: none. **Declare the assistant**: when you tap an assistant
button, that email's text is sent to Google's Gemini service to produce the answer.

## App Store — App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Data **linked to the user, not used for tracking**: Contact Info → Email Address; User
Content → Emails or Text Messages, Photos or Videos (only what is attached), Other User
Content (calendar, tasks, notes); Identifiers → Device ID (push token). Purposes: App
Functionality. **Tracking: No.** Third-party AI: tell Apple (Guideline 5.1.2) that email text
is sent to Google Gemini only when the person taps an assistant action; the privacy policy
must say the same.
