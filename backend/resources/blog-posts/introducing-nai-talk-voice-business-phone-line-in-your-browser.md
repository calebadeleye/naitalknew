## What Is NAI TALK Voice?

NAI TALK Voice gives your business a real Nigerian phone number, such as a **0201 Lagos line** or a **0209 Abuja line**. Your customers call it from any phone on any network, just like any other number.

The difference is on your side. Instead of ringing a phone on someone's desk, the call rings in the web browser of whoever on your team is online. They click to answer and talk through a headset. When they are done, they are ready for the next call.

Your staff can work from the office, from home or from a branch in another city. As long as they are signed in and online, they can take calls on your business number.

## How NAI TALK Voice Works

Getting started is simple:

1. **Get your number.** [Request a number](https://voice.naitalk.com) and tell us about your team. We set up your line and your account.
2. **Your team goes online.** Each person clicks "Go online" in the browser, either in our web phone or inside your own system. A headset is all they need.
3. **Calls find the next free person.** Each incoming call rings whoever has been free the longest. If everyone is busy, the caller holds until someone is free.

That is it. No hardware to buy, no technician to send, and nothing to install on the phones your customers use.

## Features of NAI TALK Voice

### Several people answering at once

You choose how many lines run at the same time. If you have five lines and five people online, five customers can be talking to your team at once. Add more lines as your team grows.

### Callers hold instead of hearing a busy tone

When every line is busy, extra callers are not turned away. They hear a short message asking them to hold, with music in between. As soon as someone on your team is free, the caller who has waited the longest is put through. It is a real queue, the same way a big bank or telecoms help line works.

### A five-minute limit on holding

Holding costs your customer airtime, so nobody is kept waiting forever. If a caller has held for five minutes, the call ends and is saved as a missed call, so your team can call them back.

### No charge when nobody is online

If a customer calls when nobody on your team is online, for example at night or on a public holiday, they hear a polite message: "Nobody is available to take your call at the moment. Please try your call again later." The call is not answered, so the caller is not charged for it, and you are not charged either. The call still shows up in your missed calls list.

### A missed calls list for call-backs

Every call your team could not take is listed with the caller's number and time, so nobody falls through the cracks. Your team can work down the list and call each customer back.

### Calling customers from the browser

Your team can also make calls from the browser. When they call a customer, the customer sees **your business number**, not a staff member's personal line. That is good for trust, and it keeps your customers' numbers inside the business when staff move on.

### Call recording

Calls can be recorded for training, quality checks or records. Each recording is ready right after the call and can be downloaded for 24 hours, so you can keep the ones you need in your own storage. If you do not want recordings, you can turn them off.

### Call logs and a live view

Your dashboard shows how many lines are in use, who is waiting and for how long, and every call with its length, who answered it and the caller's network. You can see at a glance how busy your line is and how your team is doing.

### Prepaid credit, charged per minute

There is no monthly bill to worry about. You top up a prepaid balance and pay a rate per minute for the calls you take and make. Your dashboard shows your balance, your usage by day and every charge, and we warn you before your credit runs low. Your team going online costs nothing; you only pay for talk time.

### A client portal for your business

Everything is managed in your own client portal: your team, your lines, your calls, your recordings, your balance and your API keys.
