## What Is Internet Radio?

Internet radio, also called online radio or web radio, is a radio station that broadcasts audio content over the internet instead of transmitting exclusively through traditional FM or AM frequencies.

Listeners can access an internet radio station using smartphones, computers, tablets, smart TVs, web browsers, Android apps, iPhone and iPad apps, and internet radio platforms and directories.

Unlike traditional FM radio, an internet radio station can potentially reach listeners anywhere in the world with an internet connection.

For example, a radio station based in Lagos, Nigeria, can have listeners in Abuja, Port Harcourt, London, New York, Johannesburg, Toronto, and other locations.

## How Does Internet Radio Work?

Understanding how online radio works is important before starting your own station. An internet radio system normally has several components working together.

### 1. Audio Content

Your content is the heart of the station. This could include music, talk shows, news, interviews, religious programs, sports discussions, educational programs, podcasts, advertisements, live events, and community programs.

You can broadcast programs live or schedule prerecorded content to play automatically.

### 2. Radio Automation Software

Radio automation software manages your station's programming. Platforms such as AzuraCast can help manage music libraries, playlists, programs, scheduling, live broadcasts, DJ accounts, station metadata, and automated playback.

With proper automation, your internet radio station can continue broadcasting even when a presenter is not physically in the studio.

### 3. Streaming Server

The streaming server is responsible for delivering your station's audio to listeners. When someone clicks Listen Live, their device connects to your streaming server and receives the audio stream.

Your server capacity and bandwidth need to be properly configured according to the number of listeners you expect.

### 4. Radio Website

A professional radio website becomes the online home of your station. It can include a Listen Live player, program schedule, presenter profiles, an about page, news, podcasts, contact information, advertising information, and social media links.

### 5. Mobile Application

You can also create Android and iOS applications for your station. Instead of requiring listeners to open a browser, they can install your app and listen directly from their phones.

## Internet Radio vs FM Radio

Internet radio and traditional FM radio operate differently.

- Distribution: internet radio travels over the internet, while FM radio travels over an FM frequency.
- Potential audience: internet radio can reach a global audience, while FM radio is mostly limited to a geographic area.
- Main infrastructure: internet radio relies on a streaming server, while FM radio relies on a transmitter and tower.
- Smartphone listening: internet radio works on any smartphone, while FM radio requires a compatible radio receiver.
- Website integration: internet radio integrates easily with a website, while FM radio needs a separate digital platform.
- Mobile application: internet radio supports a dedicated app, while FM radio treats an app as a supplement.
- Automated broadcasting: both internet radio and FM radio support automated broadcasting.
- Global accessibility: internet radio is accessible worldwide, while FM radio is limited by signal coverage.

This doesn't mean internet radio completely replaces FM radio. Instead, the two can complement each other. A traditional radio station can use the internet to extend its reach beyond its terrestrial coverage area, while an online-only station can build an audience without operating an FM transmitter.

## Can Internet Radio Make Money?

Yes. An online radio station can have several potential revenue streams.

### 1. Advertising

Businesses can pay for radio commercials, sponsored announcements, jingles, promotional segments, website advertising, and app advertising.

### 2. Program Sponsorship

Companies can sponsor individual programs. For example, "The Morning Show is proudly sponsored by XYZ Company."

### 3. Sponsored Content

Your station can create interviews, product features, discussions, and other content for sponsors.

### 4. Events

A radio station can use its audience to promote or organise physical and online events.

### 5. Donations and Listener Support

Community, religious, and nonprofit stations may use donations or listener support to help sustain their operations.

### 6. Business Partnerships

Your radio platform can become part of a broader digital media business involving podcasting, content production, advertising, social media, events, and digital publishing.

The key is to build an engaged audience rather than focusing only on the number of people who tune in.

## Internet Radio and Podcasting

Internet radio and podcasting can work together extremely well. A live radio program can be recorded and repurposed into podcast episodes.

For example, a two-hour interview program could produce a full podcast episode, short clips, social media videos, YouTube content, quote graphics, and blog articles.

This allows your content to continue generating engagement long after the original broadcast has ended. Instead of creating content once, you can build a content ecosystem around every program.
