## It Started With Webmail

For years, we provided webmail as part of our website and hosting services. When businesses came to us to build their websites, we also helped them set up professional email addresses using their own domain names.

It was practical. It worked. And it gave businesses one more reason to take their online presence seriously. (If you want the full case for it, read [why every business needs a professional email address](/blog/why-every-business-needs-a-professional-email-address).)

But as time went on, we began to ask ourselves a question.

What if business email could be more than just a place to send and receive messages?

What if it could be easier to use? What if it could help business owners stay organised, respond to customers faster, and work more efficiently? What if a business owner could manage conversations, appointments, contacts and tasks without jumping between different applications?

And what if business email was no longer something you had to purchase alongside a website or hosting package?

Because the truth is, not every business needs a new website today.

Some businesses already have a website. Some sell through social media. Some operate through referrals and WhatsApp. Some are just starting out, trying to find their feet in a challenging economy.

But all these businesses communicate.

They send quotations. They receive enquiries. They negotiate with suppliers. They speak with customers. They apply for contracts. They send invoices and follow up on payments.

And every day, those communications help determine how seriously people take their businesses.

We realised that professional email deserved a place of its own.

That was the beginning of a new chapter.

## Green Mail Questions, Answered

### Can I get Green Mail without web hosting?

Yes. Green Mail is available on its own, so you do not need a website or a hosting package from NAI TALK. You only need a domain name, whether it is registered with us or with another provider.

### Do I need to move my domain to NAI TALK?

No. You can keep your domain where it is. After you order, we send you a few DNS records (MX, SPF and DKIM) to add at your registrar. If your domain is registered with NAI TALK, our team adds them for you.

### How much does Green Mail cost?

Standalone Green Mail plans start from ₦12,000 a year. If you host your website with NAI TALK, Green Mail is already included with your hosting plan at no extra cost.

### Can I use Green Mail on my phone?

Yes. Green Mail works in your phone's browser and can be installed like an app. You can also add your mailbox to Outlook, Apple Mail or the Gmail app using the settings we send you.
