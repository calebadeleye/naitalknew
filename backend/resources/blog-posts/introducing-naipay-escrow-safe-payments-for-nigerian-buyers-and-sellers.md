## What Is Escrow?

Escrow simply means a trusted middle party holds the money while a deal is going on.

Instead of sending money straight to a seller you have never met, you pay into escrow. The seller can see that the money is there, so they know you are serious and they ship. When you receive the item and you are happy, the money is released to the seller. If something goes wrong, the money has not left yet, so it can be sorted out fairly.

Nobody has to trust a stranger. Both sides only have to trust the process.

## How NaiPay Works

### 1. The seller lists the item

The seller adds the product once, with **real photos and a video** of the item. NaiPay checks the files and refuses pictures that have been edited, made with AI or taken as screenshots, so what the buyer sees is the real thing.

The seller then shares a **NaiPay pay link** (on Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook or anywhere else), or sends an **invoice** straight to one buyer's email.

### 2. The buyer pays NaiPay

The buyer opens the link, looks at the photos and video, and pays by **bank transfer**. They do not need an account first: they enter their name, email and phone number, confirm a code sent to their email, and pay. Both buyer and seller get an alert as soon as the money arrives.

### 3. The seller ships

The seller sends the item and clicks "Shipped". Until then, the buyer can cancel and get their money back. Once the item is shipped, the buyer can no longer cancel, which protects the seller from last-minute changes of mind.

### 4. The buyer confirms delivery

The buyer checks the item. If it is what they paid for, they confirm delivery. If something is wrong, they open a dispute instead.

### 5. The seller gets paid

**24 hours** after the buyer confirms delivery, the money is released to the seller's NaiPay wallet. The seller can then withdraw it to any Nigerian bank account in their own name.

## What If Something Goes Wrong?

If the item does not arrive, is damaged, or is not what was shown, the buyer opens a dispute. The release is paused straight away, and real people on the NaiPay team look at the order, the photos and videos, and what both sides say. We then decide to release the money to the seller, refund the buyer, or let the order continue.

Because the money is held the whole time, nobody can disappear with it while the dispute is being sorted out.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the buyer need a NaiPay account?

No. A buyer can pay from a seller's link with just their name, email and phone number, and confirm a code sent to their email. An account is created for them so they can follow the order.

### How do buyers pay?

By bank transfer. Card payments are not used.

### When does the seller get paid?

24 hours after the buyer confirms delivery, the money is released to the seller's NaiPay wallet. From there, the seller can withdraw to their bank account.

### Can the buyer cancel?

Yes, until the seller marks the item as shipped. After that, the buyer can open a dispute if something is wrong, but cannot simply cancel.

### What if the buyer never confirms delivery?

The seller can open a dispute, and the NaiPay team will look at the order and decide.

### How much is the fee?

1.5% of the item price, shared equally by buyer and seller unless one of them chooses to pay all of it.
